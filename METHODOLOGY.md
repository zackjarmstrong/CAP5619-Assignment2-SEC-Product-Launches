# CAP5619 Assignment 2 - Methodology and Results

Finalized: November 2, 2026

## Research question and scope

This project identifies original announcements of completed product or distinct new service launches in SEC Form 8-K filings. The output has exactly company_name, stock_name, filing_time, new_product, and product_description; descriptions contain fewer than 180 characters. The written Assignment 2 handout and course examples guide the Python, SEC collection, LLM extraction, and reporting approach. The discovery pool spans 2020-02-05 through 2026-10-30. The sample is purposive rather than representative.

## Data collection and model specifications

The merged discovery pool contains 1417 unique filing URLs. SEC full-text searches used launch and availability phrases, including announced the launch, today launched, introduces/new products, and launches/now available. Pagination limits and retrieved document-hit counts are logged; document hits are deduplicated to filing URLs. The main 8-K and readable EX-99 exhibits are fetched with an identified User-Agent, sequential pacing, local caching, and limited transient-error retries. The request ledger records 153 new model attempts. The configured model is gpt-6-luna through OpenAI's Responses API (recorded identifiers: gpt-6-luna). Each call uses strict JSON Schema, at most 60,000 source-text characters and 10,000 output tokens. No fine-tuning or explicit temperature setting is used. Model weights and training details were not independently available. Actual token usage retained for these attempts totals 617,767 input and 56,460 output tokens; the report does not infer a dollar charge.

## Extraction and review method

Free screening ranks launch language near exhibit openings and penalizes earnings, financing, rebrand, website, and other non-product headlines. Selected filings are analyzed one per model request. Named Entity Recognition (NER) is performed by the LLM: it identifies company names as ORGANIZATION entities and newly announced products as PRODUCT entities, returning company_name and new_product within structured fields. These are semantic entity roles, not separately emitted BIO tags. No separate NER model was trained; issuer names are subsequently normalized to SEC metadata. The prompt excludes historical mentions, planned developments, prototypes, acquisitions, issuer financing, and channel expansions. It requests the source URL, a contiguous launch quote, announcement date, confidence, and rationale alongside the five data fields. Python supplies SEC's acceptance timestamp, validates the ticker and field types, rejects long descriptions, and checks the quote against supplied text. Names are deduplicated within a ticker using normalization and close-spelling comparison. An AI assistant groups strengths, configurations and suite variants rather than expanding them into extra rows, reads the original exhibit, checks the product description and current-launch context, and records an explicit source-review decision. Only source-checked or human-verified rows can enter the final CSV. The source_checked label explicitly denotes AI-assisted source review, not independent human verification.

## Observed problems and corrections

Search terms also retrieved past launches in earnings summaries and unrelated corporate announcements. High model confidence was insufficient: later paragraphs sometimes revealed beta status or future commercial availability despite a launch headline. Source review excluded these and historical recaps. Six earlier uncertain cases were reassessed; four were rejected and two remain excluded. Genius retains VRSSD: SEC exhibits document its March 2025 adoption and April 17 use, but the April 30 launch release lists VRSSF. The effective symbol on May 2 is unresolved; no current-ticker inference was used. Details and source references are in audit/genius_ticker_review.json. SEC inline HTML added spaces around punctuation; quote comparison normalizes this without changing words. Some model quotes omitted intervening text and some descriptions were too long. Documented source-based corrections preserve raw responses and require another offline check. An attached launch release may report initial shipments before SEC acceptance; historical recaps are excluded.
